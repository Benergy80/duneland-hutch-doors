Installation

    ChiLab supplies the bottom pivot plates and Benchmark builds them into the underside of the upper box before the cabinet goes in. The pivot pins are built into the door frames. On site, each door is lowered onto its bottom plate, then the top plate goes in from above over the top pin. Nothing has to be reached from underneath. Click a step to jump to it.

    The doors

    Four doors, each a solid brass frame around 1/8" bronze-tinted glass, about 56 lb. The glass sits on two small plastic setting strips, cushioned by black foam tape, and is held from the back by a removable brass bar, so it can be changed on site. The Glazing bar view shows the bar and its screws. Doors 1 and 4 pivot at the outer ends of the hutch; doors 2 and 3 pivot side by side at the center and open away from each other. Each door turns on pins built into its frame, set in bronze plates at the top and bottom of the cabinet.

    Closed, the doors keep a 1/4" gap between them and sit 1/8" in front of the cabinet. The readout shows the smallest gap anywhere on the doors at the angles you set.
